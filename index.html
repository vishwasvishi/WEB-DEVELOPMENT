Virat Kohli

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen in the history of cricket. Kohli has numerous records to his name, including being the fastest player to score 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, and 11,000 runs in One Day Internationals (ODIs). He has also been awarded the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times.

Early Life and Career

Virat Kohli was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India. He showed an early interest in cricket and joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy at a young age. Kohli made his debut for the Indian national team in 2008 and quickly established himself as a key player in the team.

Personal Life

Virat Kohli is married to Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma. The couple has a daughter named Vamika. Kohli is known for his fitness regime and has been an advocate for a healthy lifestyle.
